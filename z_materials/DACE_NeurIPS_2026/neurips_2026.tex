% 中文对应内容：LLM 安全对齐必须应对持续演化的攻击，现有静态对齐受限于预分布。最近的协同演化方法让攻防双方同步进步，但我们发现它们暴露两个互相加强的病态：攻击策略坍缩使分布狭窄；防御对抗遗忘使旧漏洞重现。为此我们提出 DACE，在协同演化框架上引入显式的二维攻击策略空间和归一化边际覆盖增益奖励，并构建基于 Beta-Bernoulli 后验+时间衰减+Thompson 采样的贝叶斯对抗回放池。前者驱动攻击者结构化地覆盖整个策略空间，后者让防御者持续保留对历史攻击的抵御能力。我们在多个主流安全基准与 SOTA 自动红队工具上验证 DACE 的防御鲁棒性、通用能力无退化与攻击者的覆盖广度，并释出代码与策略空间档案以供社区继续研究。
\begin{abstract}
Ensuring robust safety alignment of large language models (LLMs) has become a persistent challenge as adversarial attacks evolve from static templates to automated, compositional, and agentic exploitations. Recent co-evolutionary frameworks let the defender chase a moving attacker, but we identify two compounding pathologies that limit their long-term robustness: \textit{attack strategy collapse}, where the reinforcement-learned attacker over-fits to a narrow set of high-reward rewrites; and \textit{defense adversarial forgetting}, where the defender gradually loses competence against earlier attacks as the attack distribution drifts. We introduce \method, a diversity-driven adversarial co-evolution framework that jointly addresses both pathologies. \method organizes the attack space with an explicit two-dimensional strategy grid ($12\times10$, spanning safety risk categories and linguistic attack styles) and optimizes the attacker with a \emph{normalized marginal coverage gain} reward that provides a bounded, non-vanishing signal toward under-explored regions. To prevent forgetting, \method maintains a unified Bayesian adversarial replay pool: every successful attack carries a Beta--Bernoulli posterior over its current threat, is refreshed by time decay and Thompson sampling, and is pruned only when repeatedly dismissed by an up-to-date defender. Across safety, general-capability, and strong-adversarial-attack benchmarks, \method attains lower attack success rates than state-of-the-art co-evolution baselines without sacrificing utility, while its attacker discovers markedly broader strategy coverage. We release the code and the strategy-indexed adversarial replay pool to support further study of diversity-aware safety alignment.
\end{abstract}
