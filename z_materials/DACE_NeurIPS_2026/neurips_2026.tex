\begin{abstract}
Ensuring robust safety alignment of large language models (LLMs) is increasingly difficult because adversarial attacks evolve, outpacing alignment pipelines that rely on pre-collected distributions. Recent co-evolutionary frameworks let the defender chase a moving attacker, but the resulting training dynamics expose two compounding pathologies: \emph{attack strategy collapse}, where the reinforcement-learned attacker concentrates on a narrow set of high-reward rewrites, and \emph{defense adversarial forgetting}, where the defender gradually loses competence against earlier attacks as the attack distribution drifts. Existing remedies fall short. Diversity-oriented red-teaming either relies on static targets or rewards superficial textual novelty, allowing attackers to bypass the diversity signal without changing their underlying strategy. Replay mechanisms in co-evolutionary self-play often depend on discrete safety scores that fail to capture estimation uncertainty, response stochasticity, or the non-stationarity of the defender. We introduce \method, a diversity-driven adversarial co-evolution framework that addresses these two gaps jointly. On the attacker side, \method couples an explicit two-dimensional attack strategy space (risk category $\times$ attack style) with a \emph{normalized marginal coverage gain} reward, providing a stable, non-vanishing signal toward under-explored regions. On the defender side, \method maintains a unified \emph{Bayesian adversarial replay pool} where Beta--Bernoulli threat posteriors are refreshed by time decay and Thompson sampling, ensuring that defender training is continuously anchored to an evolving threat landscape. We evaluate \method on standard safety, strong automated-attacker, multi-turn, and general-capability benchmarks. Results show \method significantly improves defender safety robustness against out-of-distribution attacks without degrading general capabilities, while the attacker achieves broad strategy coverage. We release the code and the strategy-indexed adversarial replay pool to support further research.

% Robust safety alignment of large language models (LLMs) is challenged by adaptive attacks that shift faster than alignment data can be collected. Existing approaches address these issues only partially: diversity rewards often capture surface-form novelty rather than strategy-level exploration, while replay buffers typically rely on point-valued safety scores that ignore uncertainty, response stochasticity, and defender non-stationarity. \end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

% [段 1 · Hook] 大意：以攻击不断升级作为开篇钩子，类似 MAGIC 开头但聚焦 DACE 的问题面。
% 撰写逻辑：部署广泛 → 攻击演化（DAN/GCG/PAIR/TAP/AutoDAN/X-Teaming/MAJIC）→ 静态对齐落后 → 要求动态防御。
% 中文对应内容：LLM 已经被广泛部署，但威胁形式不断升级：从早期人工越狱模板，到梯度优化、LLM 迭代改写，再到多轮 agent 化攻击。依赖预收集分布的 RLHF、guardrail 和静态 red-team 数据集只能覆盖已见过的攻击，面对分布漂移的对手结构性失守。
Large language models (LLMs) are being widely deployed across high-stakes applications, yet their safety alignment is repeatedly outpaced by an accelerating threat landscape: from hand-crafted jailbreak templates~\citep{shen2023doanythingnow} to gradient-based optimization~\citep{zou2023universal}, LLM-driven iterative rewriting~\citep{chao2023pair, mehrotra2024tap, liu2023autodan}, and multi-turn agentic exploitations~\citep{rahman2025xteaming, qi2025majic, samvelyan2024rainbow}. Each wave introduces qualitatively new composition patterns absent from prior red-teaming corpora. Against such non-stationary adversaries, alignment pipelines that depend on a pre-collected distribution---be it RLHF preferences~\citep{ouyang2022training, bai2022constitutional, dai2023safe}, fixed guardrails, or static red-team datasets~\citep{jiang2024wildteaming, xie2024sorry}---are structurally ill-suited: they can harden the defender only against the slice of attacks already collected, while the attack distribution keeps moving.

% [段 2 · 协同演化 + 双病态] 大意：介绍协同演化 paradigm，然后点出训练动态中的两大病态并命名。这里用 "persistence" / "replay" 的口径，去掉 "memory"，与用户要求对齐。
% 撰写逻辑：Self-RedTeam / MAGIC / AdvEvo-MARL / SSP / TriPlay-RL 协同演化线 → "都在进步"不等于"覆盖广 + 持续鲁棒" → 命名两个病态 → 指出互相加强。
% 中文对应内容：近期协同演化框架把 attacker 和 defender 放到同一个 RL 循环中同步进步，证明了协同演化可以跟上动态攻击。但"都在进步"不等于"攻击真的覆盖完整的威胁空间"且"防御持续对旧攻击鲁棒"。训练动态中会出现两个互相加强的病态：攻击策略坍缩，attacker 过拟合到少数高回报模式；防御对抗性遗忘，defender 随攻击分布漂移而丢失对旧攻击的拒答能力。坍缩的攻击会训出偏科的防御，偏科的防御又反过来强化坍缩的策略。
To address this non-stationarity, recent work couples an attacker LLM and a defender LLM in an adversarial co-evolutionary game, showing that the defender can keep up with a drifting attacker under suitable equilibrium notions~\citep{liu2025chasing, magic2025, pan2025advevo, ssp2026, triplayrl2026}. However, ``both sides keep improving'' is a weaker property than ``attacks cover the true threat space and defenses \emph{stay} robust to past threats.'' In practice, we observe that RL-based co-evolution exhibits two compounding pathologies. First, \textbf{attack strategy collapse}: after the early exploration phase, the attacker overfits to a narrow set of high-reward rewrite patterns, so the distribution of its successful attacks becomes heavily skewed and the defender is trained on a biased slice of the threat landscape~\citep{samvelyan2024rainbow, hong2024curiosity}. Second, \textbf{defense adversarial forgetting}: as the attacker drifts into new regions, the defender's competence on \emph{earlier} attack patterns silently decays~\citep{ssp2026}, echoing catastrophic forgetting in continual learning. The two pathologies reinforce each other---a collapsed attacker trains a narrow defender, and a narrow defender pushes the attacker back into the same easy regions---so standard co-evolution, left untreated, converges to a shallow equilibrium that systematically underestimates the true safety gap.

% [段 3 · Gap 分析（关键差异化段落）：拆成 diversity 与 replay 两小段，按用户要求降低认知负荷并修正 SSP 表述。
% 撰写逻辑：3A Diversity gap（QD / RL-diversity / TriPlay-RL 的语义层局限）+ 3B Replay gap（SSP 真的在实时更新，但依赖离散 safety score，无不确定性/偶然性/漂移建模）。
% 中文对应内容：既有工作试图解决这两大病态，但多是"把静态机制嫁接到动态系统"。多样性方面，自动红队中的 QD 流派针对静态目标构造 archive；RL 多样性奖励停留在文本/嵌入层面，attacker 换措辞即可绕过。TriPlay-RL 是协同演化中最接近的 diversity 先验工作，但只用 Self-BLEU + cosine 做语义约束，改文不改策略。回放方面，SSP 确实会在回放后更新样本威胁估计，但依赖离散 safety score，无法表达估计不确定性，对 defender 单次拒答的偶然性敏感，也没有显式遗忘旧信息的机制。
Existing attempts to mitigate these pathologies largely graft static mechanisms onto an inherently dynamic system, and split into two structurally incomplete directions. 

\textbf{The Diversity Gap.} One family inherits the quality-diversity (QD) search paradigm from automated red-teaming, constructing fixed behavior-descriptor archives against a \emph{static} target~\citep{samvelyan2024rainbow, han2024ruby, pala2025ferret, dang2025rainbowplus, wang2025qdrt}. While these methods generate diverse prompts, they fail to adapt to an evolving defender. Another family attaches intrinsic-diversity rewards to RL attackers~\citep{hong2024curiosity, zhao2025diverct, lee2025gflownet, wang2025jailbreakr1, beutel2024openaidiverse} but measures diversity at the textual or embedding level (e.g., Self-BLEU, cosine distance), so the attacker can satisfy the diversity signal merely by rephrasing without adopting novel attack strategies. Closest to us, TriPlay-RL~\citep{triplayrl2026} explicitly injects diversity inside a co-evolutionary loop, yet its diversity term is a composition of Self-BLEU and sentence-embedding cosine similarity---purely semantic-surface constraints. Attackers can therefore pass the diversity filter by varying wording while reusing the same strategy, i.e.\ ``new skin, same trick.''

\textbf{The Replay Gap.} On the defense side, SSP~\citep{ssp2026} correctly recognizes the forgetting problem and maintains a replay pool whose per-sample harm scores are refreshed on each replay. Crucially, however, its update rule is driven by a discrete safety score from the judge and therefore lacks three modeling axes needed under a drifting defender: the update cannot represent \emph{estimation uncertainty} (few-trial and many-trial samples are aggregated into the same integer tally), it is sensitive to the \emph{stochasticity} of a single defender response (one lucky refusal due to sampling temperature can prematurely deprioritize a still-dangerous attack), and it provides no \emph{forgetting} mechanism for \emph{non-stationarity} (stale evidence keeps contaminating current threat estimates). Taken together, these observations show that current methods fail to jointly address attack strategy collapse and defense adversarial forgetting in a dynamic, co-evolutionary setting.

\section{Problem Formalization}
\label{sec:formalization}

% [段 2.1 · 继承 MAGIC 博弈形式化] 大意：复用 MAGIC 的非对称序贯博弈定义，但只引用而不主张继承 SPNE 保证；DACE 的新增机制是 training-time regularization / data selection。
% 撰写逻辑：简述 attacker π_A 与 defender π_D 的序贯交互和奖励结构 → 说明 DACE 在此之上引入 training-time 机制（而非改博弈结构）→ 为下一段双病态形式化做铺垫。
% 中文对应内容：我们沿用 MAGIC 的非对称序贯博弈形式化：attacker 给出改写 x，defender 在观察到 x 后给出响应 y_D，双方的奖励在有效性维度上构成零和结构。DACE 不修改该博弈结构，而是在 attacker 的奖励中加入多样性正则、在 defender 的训练数据分布中加入贝叶斯回放池，这些都是 training-time 机制，用于改进向均衡路径收敛的训练动态。
We inherit the asymmetric sequential-game formalization of MAGIC~\citep{magic2025}. Let $q$ denote a seed prompt, $x \sim \piA(\cdot\mid q)$ the attacker's rewrite, and $y_D \sim \piD(\cdot\mid x)$ the defender's response. The two players receive rewards $r_A(x, y_D)$ and $r_D(x, y_D)$ with a zero-sum structure on the effectiveness component: an attack that elicits harm from the defender is advantageous to the attacker, and vice versa. \method does not alter the interaction protocol; rather, our additions are \emph{training-time} mechanisms---a strategy-level diversity reward added to the attacker's objective and a Bayesian adversarial replay pool that reshapes the defender's training distribution---that aim to improve the training dynamics of converging toward the equilibrium path.

% [段 2.2 · 形式化两大病态] 大意：把 intro 中双病态以数学方式明示，定义 collapse 与 forgetting 的可测量指标；采用用户/GPT 意见修正，不用 "<<"、"non-increasing" 等过强限定。
% 撰写逻辑：用策略描述符 b(x) ∈ B 投影到策略空间分布 p_{π_A,t}；collapse = 归一化熵低于阈值（或 max-cell share 高于阈值）；forgetting = 差值指标 F(t_0, t) 为正且显著。这样指标都是可测可验证的。
% 中文对应内容：给定策略描述符 b(x) ∈ B，令 p_{π_A,t} 是 attacker 在训练步 t 诱导的策略分布，令 H_{t_0} 是 attacker 在较早训练步 t_0 采样的攻击集合。定义（1）攻击策略坍缩：若归一化熵 H(p_{π_A,t}) / log|B| 持续显著小于 1（或等价地 max-cell share 持续高于 threshold），则存在坍缩；（2）防御对抗性遗忘：若 F(t_0, t) = ASR_t(H_{t_0}) - ASR_{t_0}(H_{t_0}) 显著为正，则 defender 在步 t 相较 t_0 对旧攻击的防御能力发生了退化。这两条都是 co-evolutionary 训练动态中的联合病态，DACE 的两个机制分别针对它们。
We give a compact formalization of the two pathologies \method is designed to mitigate. Let $b(\cdot)$ map an attack to its strategy descriptor in the $12\times10$ grid $\strategyspace$ (Section~\ref{sec:method-space}), and let $\mathbf{p}_{\piA,t}$ denote the marginal distribution induced by $\piA$ over $\strategyspace$ at training step $t$. Let $\mathcal{H}_{t_0}$ be the set of attacks sampled from $\piA$ at an earlier training step $t_0 < t$, and let $\mathrm{ASR}_t(x)$ be the probability that $\piD$ at step $t$ produces an unsafe response to $x$.

\begin{definition}[Attack Strategy Collapse]
\label{def:collapse}
The attacker exhibits \emph{strategy collapse} at training step $t$ when the normalized entropy falls below a critical threshold $\tau_{\mathrm{collapse}} \in (0, 1)$ and does not recover, i.e., $H(\mathbf{p}_{\piA,t}) / \log|\strategyspace| < \tau_{\mathrm{collapse}}$; equivalently, when the maximum per-cell mass $\max_{b\in\strategyspace}\mathbf{p}_{\piA,t}(b)$ remains structurally concentrated above the uniform expectation $1/|\strategyspace|$.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Defense Adversarial Forgetting]
\label{def:forgetting}
The defender exhibits \emph{adversarial forgetting} between steps $t_0$ and $t > t_0$ if the net forgetting statistic $F(t_0, t) = \mathbb{E}_{x\in\mathcal{H}_{t_0}}[\mathrm{ASR}_t(x) - \mathrm{ASR}_{t_0}(x)]$ satisfies $F(t_0, t) > \delta_{\mathrm{forget}}$ for some tolerance $\delta_{\mathrm{forget}} > 0$.
\end{definition}

\subsection{Joint Optimization Objective}

These two definitions isolate, respectively, the \emph{coverage} property on the attacker's side and the \emph{persistence} property on the defender's side. They couple through the shared training loop: a collapsed attacker fuels a forgetful defender, and a forgetful defender rewards a collapsed attacker. A principled remedy must therefore treat them as a constrained joint optimization problem. 

Ideally, we seek an equilibrium where the attacker maximizes its reward subject to a strict lower bound on strategy entropy, and the defender minimizes its loss on the current attack distribution while subject to a penalty on the forgetting statistic over historical distributions:
\begin{align}
  \max_{\piA} \quad & \mathbb{E}_{x\sim\piA, y_D\sim\piD} [R_A(x, y_D)] \quad \text{s.t.} \quad \frac{H(\mathbf{p}_{\piA})}{\log|\strategyspace|} \ge \tau, \\
  \min_{\piD} \quad & \mathbb{E}_{x\sim\piA, y_D\sim\piD} [-R_D(x, y_D)] + \eta \max_{t_0 < t} F(t_0, t).
\end{align}

Directly solving this constrained joint optimization is computationally intractable in deep RL. The core contribution of \method is to provide an elegant and computationally cheap engineering approximation to this objective. \method's attacker-side mechanism (Section~\ref{sec:method-attacker}) directly lifts the strategy entropy via a normalized KL-contraction reward, while the defender-side mechanism (Section~\ref{sec:method-replay}) bounds the forgetting statistic $F(t_0, t)$ through a Bayesian adversarial replay pool; the two mechanisms are coupled by a single archive that both read from and write to.

\subsection{Training Process}
\label{sec:method-training}

% [段 3.1.0 · MAGIC 式总述] 大意：在两阶段展开之前给一个 2-3 句的 bridge，交代"攻击侧先练、然后在 RL 里让 attacker 先手、defender 后手混合 batch"这一闭环。借鉴 MAGIC 的总述写法，让读者先看到整体骨架。
% 中文对应内容：我们把 DACE 的训练刻画为 attacker $\piA$ 与 defender $\piD$ 之间的对抗序贯博弈，分为两阶段：阶段一以策略条件化的 CoT 模仿学习热启动攻击者，使其具备基本的策略选择与结构化改写能力；阶段二以 attacker 先手、defender 后手的迭代方式执行 GRPO 强化学习，并用统一档案池 A 把多样性激励与对抗回放串联成闭环。图 2 给出完整流程。
We cast \method's safety alignment as an adversarial sequential game between the attacker $\piA$ and the defender $\piD$, and split the training procedure into two phases, illustrated in Figure~\ref{fig:pipeline}: \textbf{(1)} \emph{Attack Strategy Warm-up via Imitation Learning}, which equips the attacker with strategy-conditioned rewriting before any RL begins, and \textbf{(2)} \emph{Diversity-Driven Adversarial Co-Evolution}, which alternates attacker and defender updates under GRPO while a shared archive $\archive$ couples strategy-level diversity with Bayesian replay.

% [段 3.1.1 · Phase 1 (SFT)] 大意：重新标题 Phase 1: Attack Strategy Warm-up via Imitation Learning，突出"策略条件化的模仿学习"而不是 SFT 本身。
% 中文对应内容：Phase 1——攻击策略模仿学习热启动：直接用 instruct base 做 attacker 对 harmful 种子会高概率拒答，也无法可靠声明策略。我们借鉴 MAGIC 的 SFT 热启动思路，将其升级为策略条件化的 CoT 模仿学习：每条 SFT 样本要求 attacker 同时输出思维链、显式选定的 (s, c) 策略和改写 prompt，三段落以 <think>/<strategy>/<answer> 标记分隔。SFT 的极大似然目标把攻击者初始化到 12×10 网格上的均匀覆盖，为后续 RL 中覆盖奖励的稳定梯度提供前提。
\textbf{Phase 1: Attack Strategy Warm-up via Imitation Learning.}\quad
Following~\citet{magic2025}, we observe that directly employing an instruct-tuned base model as the attacker produces prohibitively high refusal rates on harmful seeds, and that it cannot yet declare a strategy descriptor $(s, c)$ reliably. To resolve this cold-start problem, we warm-start $\piA$ with supervised fine-tuning (SFT) on a \emph{strategy-conditioned CoT} dataset in which every example requires the attacker to emit a reasoning trace, an explicit attack strategy $(s, c) \in \strategyspace$, and a structured rewrite, all separated by \texttt{<think>}/\texttt{<strategy>}/\texttt{<answer>} tags (Section~\ref{sec:method-space}). Writing $\mathcal{D}_{\text{sft}}$ for the SFT dataset, the warm-start objective is the standard negative log-likelihood
\begin{equation}
  \mathcal{L}_{\text{SFT}}(\piA) \;=\; -\,\mathbb{E}_{(q, y_A) \sim \mathcal{D}_{\text{sft}}}\bigl[\log \piA(y_A \mid q)\bigr].
\end{equation}
This step equips the attacker with format compliance, strategy selection, and initial coverage over the $12\times10$ grid---crucial for the subsequent RL phase, where coverage-based rewards on low-frequency cells would otherwise provide unreliable gradients. Dataset construction details are deferred to Section~\ref{sec:exp-data} and Appendix~\ref{apdx:prompts}.

% [段 3.1.2 · Phase 2 alternating RL] 大意：严格对齐 pseudocode v3.1——attacker 先手 (Stage 1)，defender 后手混合 batch (Stage 2)；强调档案池在两阶段结束时都接收新的成功样本。
% 中文对应内容：Phase 2——多样性驱动的对抗协同演化：采用 GRPO 进行参数更新，每一轮先做 Stage 1 attacker optimization 再做 Stage 2 defender optimization，两阶段共享并持续维护统一档案池 A。(i) Stage 1: 固定 π_D，对 batch 中每个 seed，attacker 生成 G 个策略条件化 rewrite，defender 给出单条响应，根据多样性感知复合奖励 R_A 更新 π_A；阶段末把本阶段新成功的 rewrite 插入 A。(ii) Stage 2: 固定 π_A，defender 的每个 mini-batch 由两部分混合——新攻击 D_new 和回放攻击 D_replay（通过 Thompson 采样从 A 抽取），defender 对其中每个 prompt 生成 G 个响应，按 R_D 在混合 batch 上统一更新 π_D，并对 D_replay 内条目做后验更新与即时剪枝；阶段末把本阶段新成功的攻击再次入池。每轮开始时对 A 内所有条目施加时间衰减以处理 defender 漂移。完整伪代码见附录 A。
\textbf{Phase 2: Diversity-Driven Adversarial Co-Evolution.}\quad
We optimize both policies with Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)~\citep{shao2024grpo, guo2025deepseek}. Each round $k=1,\dots,K$ begins with time decay applied to all archive posteriors (Section~\ref{sec:method-replay}) and then alternates two stages, \emph{attacker first}:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*, topsep=2pt, itemsep=2pt]
  \item \textit{Stage 1 --- Attacker optimization (fix $\piD$).} For each seed in a mini-batch, $\piA$ generates $G$ structured rewrites; the frozen defender produces one response per rewrite, which a safety judge scores for success; $\piA$ is updated by GRPO using the composite attacker reward $R_A$ (Section~\ref{sec:method-reward}) that combines the zero-sum effectiveness term with the coverage bonus $R_{\mathrm{div}}$.
  \item \textit{Stage 2 --- Defender optimization (fix $\piA$) on a mixed batch.} The defender is trained on a \emph{mixed} mini-batch $\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{train}}=\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{new}}\cup\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{replay}}$, where $\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{new}}$ (size $B_{\mathrm{train}}$) holds freshly generated attacks from $\piA$ on new seeds, and $\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{replay}}$ (size $B_{\mathrm{replay}}$) is drawn from $\archive$ by Thompson sampling. $\piD$ produces $G$ rollouts per prompt and is updated in a single GRPO step on the concatenated group. After the update, posteriors of entries in $\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{replay}}$ are refreshed and confidently neutralized entries are pruned.
\end{itemize}
At the end of both stages, every new successful attack is inserted into $\archive$ with its strategy descriptor $b(x)$ and initial counts $(s{=}1, f{=}0)$, so both sides continuously supply new entries for multiplexing. The full pseudocode is given in Appendix~\ref{apdx:algorithm}.

\subsection{Explicit Attack Strategy Space}
\label{sec:method-space}

% [段 3.2.1 · 策略空间定义 + 来源 + 剪裁] 大意：清楚说明 S 和 C 的来源以及剪裁证据。
% 中文对应内容：我们把攻击空间结构化为二维离散网格 B = S × C，|S|=12、|C|=10，共 120 个策略槽位。S 取自 Llama-Guard-4 的安全风险 taxonomy：原 14 类中，我们剪掉 Sexual Content（benign prompt 被 Guard 误判为 unsafe 的比例过高，使多样性信号不可靠）和 Code Interpreter Abuse（概念指向工具调用与代码执行表面，不适用于纯文本 defender）。剩余 12 类对应 Llama-Guard-4 的 S1-S11 + S13。C 取自 Rainbow Teaming 的 10 种攻击风格（Slang / Technical Terms / Role Play / Authority Manipulation / Misspellings / Word Play / Emotional Manipulation / Hypotheticals / Historical Scenario / Uncommon Dialects）。剪裁证据与每个类别的映射详见附录 B。
We formalize the attacker's action space along two interpretable axes:
\begin{equation}
  \strategyspace \;=\; \riskspace \times \stylespace, \qquad |\riskspace|=12,\ \ |\stylespace|=10,\ \ |\strategyspace|=120.
\end{equation}
The risk-category axis $\riskspace$ is adapted from the Llama-Guard-4 safety taxonomy~\citep{llamaguard4_2026} with two categories pruned for reliability (\textit{Sexual Content} and \textit{Code Interpreter Abuse}; justification in Appendix~\ref{apdx:space-design}), and the style axis $\stylespace$ adopts the 10 linguistic attack styles of Rainbow Teaming~\citep{samvelyan2024rainbow} without modification. Each attack $x$ is mapped to its strategy descriptor $b(x)=(s, c)\in\strategyspace$.

The attacker is prompted to emit a structured three-part response $\langle\texttt{<think>},\texttt{<strategy>},\texttt{<answer>}\rangle$, where the \texttt{<strategy>} block declares the chosen $(s, c)$. We extract $b(x)$ from the attacker's own declaration via regex with a fuzzy fallback, avoiding an auxiliary LLM judge and the circular dependency it would induce. Because self-declared descriptors are in principle open to reward hacking, we combine three safeguards: the SFT warm-start teaches declared strategy to match the realized text; the format reward $R_{\mathrm{fmt}}$ (Section~\ref{sec:method-reward}) penalizes malformed or out-of-grid declarations; and a post-hoc descriptor-faithfulness audit is reported in Appendix~\ref{apdx:expanded-results}. Full prompt templates are provided in Appendix~\ref{apdx:prompts}.

\subsection{Diversity-Aware Attacker Optimization}
\label{sec:method-attacker}

% [段 3.3.1 · R_raw 与 reward vanishing] 精简为一句 + 公式 + 一句。
Let $\archivedist$ be the empirical descriptor distribution in $\archive$ and $H(\cdot)$ the Shannon entropy. A naive strategy-level diversity signal is the raw marginal entropy gain
\begin{equation}
  R_{\mathrm{raw}}(x) \;=\; H(\mathbf{p}_{\archive\cup\{x\}})\ -\ H(\archivedist),
\end{equation}
but its magnitude is $\mathcal{O}(1/|\archive|)$, so both the reward and its within-group differences vanish as training proceeds.

% [段 3.3.2 · R_div 定义 + clip + KL 附录指针] 压缩讨论，合并 KL 一句。
To recover a non-vanishing signal, we normalize by the best single-sample entropy gain attainable at the current archive state:
\begin{equation}
  R_{\mathrm{div}}(x) \;=\;
  \max\!\left(0,\ \ \frac{H(\mathbf{p}_{\archive\cup\{x\}})\ -\ H(\archivedist)}{\displaystyle\max_{b\in\strategyspace}\!\bigl[H(\mathbf{p}_{\archive\cup\{x_b\}})-H(\archivedist)\bigr]\;+\;\varepsilon_{\mathrm{div}}}\right),
  \label{eq:coverage-gain}
\end{equation}
where $x_b$ is a hypothetical sample falling in cell $b$ and $\varepsilon_{\mathrm{div}}>0$ is a smoothing constant. Numerator and denominator decay at the same $\mathcal{O}(1/|\archive|)$ rate, so the ratio stays $\mathcal{O}(1)$ throughout training, and a sample landing in the sparsest cell earns $R_{\mathrm{div}}=1$. We clip negative gains to zero in the main experiments (ablated in Tab.~\ref{tab:ablation-full}). Information-theoretically, $R_{\mathrm{div}}(x)$ is the fraction of one-step KL contraction toward the uniform strategy prior that $x$ achieves, so maximising it directly addresses Definition~\ref{def:collapse} (derivation in Appendix~\ref{apdx:kl-contraction}).

% [段 3.3.3 · λ(x)] 压缩。
To reward diversity only when it carries threat signal, the coverage term is gated by a success-aware coefficient:
\begin{equation}
  \lambda(x) \;=\; \begin{cases} 1.0 & \text{if } x \text{ succeeds},\\[2pt] 0.5 & \text{otherwise}, \end{cases}
  \label{eq:lambda}
\end{equation}
where success means an unsafe response on harmful seeds and an over-refusal (or erroneous unsafe content) on benign seeds. The $2{:}1$ ratio makes successful rewrites twice as attractive under GRPO's group-relative advantage while preserving a positive exploration gradient on under-exercised cells, which a $\lambda=0$ schedule would eliminate.

% ----------------------------------------------------------
\subsection{Bayesian Adversarial Replay for Defender}
\label{sec:method-replay}

% [段 3.4.1 · 引入混合 batch + unified archive + 回应 Def 2] 大意：最先告诉读者 defender 的 batch 到底长什么样，然后把"档案池"的物理结构交代清楚。
% 中文对应内容：为缓解 Def 2 的防御对抗性遗忘，我们让 defender 的每个 mini-batch 由两部分混合而成：D_new（size B_train）是 attacker 在新种子上实时生成的攻击；D_replay（size B_replay）是从统一档案池 A 中通过 Thompson 采样抽取的历史高威胁攻击。两者共享同一个 GRPO 更新步骤，使 defender 同时获得"跟上当前 attacker"与"不遗忘历史 attacker"的梯度信号。A 中的每个条目形式为 (x_i, b(x_i), s_i, f_i)，两项计数 (s_i, f_i) 作为 Beta-Bernoulli 后验的衰减伪计数；attacker 与 defender 两侧的成功攻击在各自 stage 结束时都会入池。
To mitigate defense adversarial forgetting (Definition~\ref{def:forgetting}), \method trains the defender on a \emph{mixed} mini-batch
\begin{equation}
  \mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{train}} \;=\; \mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{new}} \;\cup\; \mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{replay}}, \qquad |\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{new}}|=B_{\mathrm{train}},\ \ |\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{replay}}|=B_{\mathrm{replay}},
  \label{eq:mixed-batch}
\end{equation}
where $\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{new}}$ is freshly generated by $\piA$ on new seeds, $\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{replay}}$ is drawn from a \emph{unified archive} $\archive$, and GRPO's per-prompt group-relative advantage keeps the update stable despite the systematic difficulty gap between the two subsets. Each entry in $\archive$ stores an attack $x_i$, its strategy descriptor $b(x_i)$, and two non-negative counts $(s_i, f_i)$ that parameterize a threat posterior; successful rewrites from either Stage~1 or Stage~2 are inserted at stage end.

% [段 3.4.2 · Threat posterior + time decay 融合段] 大意：把 posterior + time decay 压成一段，说明为什么要贝叶斯、为什么要衰减；SSP 对照简短点到。
% 中文对应内容：我们把每次回放视为 latent 威胁概率 p_i 的一次 Bernoulli 观测，取 Beta 共轭先验（α=β=1），得到解析 filtering 后验 p_i | s_i, f_i ~ Beta(α+s_i, β+f_i)；每次 defender 判决后通过共轭更新推进。这个连续的后验提供了估计不确定性（方差）和多次试验聚合，天然对抗单次采样噪声——与 SSP 的离散 safety score 相比，我们既不混淆 few-trial 与 many-trial 的置信度，也不会被 defender 的随机拒答偶然误导。为处理 defender 的非平稳性，每轮开始时对所有条目施加指数时间衰减 (s_i, f_i) ← (γ s_i, γ f_i)，在折扣伪计数意义下把旧证据缓慢遗忘回先验，使 p̂_i 自然追随当前 defender 的能力水平并重新扩大方差。
We treat each replay of $x_i$ as a Bernoulli observation of a latent threat probability $p_i$, with a Beta($\alpha=\beta=1$) conjugate prior giving the closed-form filtering posterior
\begin{equation}
  p_i \mid s_i, f_i \;\sim\; \mathrm{Beta}(\alpha+s_i,\,\beta+f_i), \qquad
  \hat p_i \;=\; \frac{\alpha+s_i}{\alpha+\beta+s_i+f_i}.
  \label{eq:posterior}
\end{equation}
After each replay, $s_i$ or $f_i$ is incremented by the conjugate step; at the start of every round, all counts are exponentially discounted $(s_i, f_i) \leftarrow (\gamma s_i, \gamma f_i)$ with $\gamma \in [0.9, 0.99]$ to track defender non-stationarity. The continuous posterior aggregates all trials and quantifies estimation uncertainty through its variance.

% [段 3.4.3 · Thompson sampling 选择 D_replay + pruning 融合段] 大意：保留 Thompson 公式作为 Eq.12；pruning 说明压缩为一句话 + 附录指针。
% 中文对应内容：在每个 defender 训练步构造 D_replay 时，我们对 A 中每个条目独立从其后验采样一次，按 p̃_i 降序取前 B_replay 个。后验均值高且方差小的"验证过的高威胁"样本稳定抽到高值（利用），试验次数少、方差大的样本也有机会抽到高值被重新验证（探索）；这一过程无需额外超参，随后验稳定会平滑退化为贪婪 top-B_replay。档案规模通过保守剪枝约束：当 p̂_i < τ_prune 且 s_i+f_i > n_min 时移除 x_i（默认 τ_prune=0.45、n_min=2），既避免单次幸运拒答引发过早淘汰，也避免低试验条目被过早剪枝；完整敏感性分析见附录 G。
% \paragraph{Thompson-sampled replay and archive maintenance.}
When the defender needs $B_{\mathrm{replay}}$ historical attacks, we draw one posterior sample per entry and retain the top of them:
\begin{equation}
  \tilde p_i \;\sim\; \mathrm{Beta}(\alpha+s_i,\,\beta+f_i), \qquad
  \mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{replay}} \;=\; \operatorname{Top-}B_{\mathrm{replay}}\bigl(\{\tilde p_i\}\bigr).
  \label{eq:thompson}
\end{equation}
High-mean, low-variance entries are reliably selected (\emph{exploit}); high-variance entries occasionally surface for re-examination (\emph{explore}), yielding a parameter-free exploration--exploitation trade-off. To bound the archive size, an entry is pruned only when $\hat p_i < \tau_{\mathrm{prune}}$ \emph{and} $s_i+f_i > n_{\min}$, so a single lucky refusal cannot evict a low-trial but potentially dangerous attack. Full derivations, default values ($\tau_{\mathrm{prune}}=0.45$, $n_{\min}=2$, $\gamma=0.90$), and sensitivity analyses around the Bayesian hyperparameters $(\gamma, \tau_{\mathrm{prune}}, n_{\min})$ are provided in Appendices~\ref{apdx:bayesian-replay} and~\ref{apdx:training-dynamics}.

\subsection{Reward Design}
\label{sec:method-reward}

% [段 3.5 · 全部奖励分量] 精简叙述，去除冗余 MAGIC 提及，R_fmt / R_harm / R_ref / R_div 四项逐一给出。
\method combines four reward components: format, harmfulness, and refusal attached to the co-evolution interaction, plus the new coverage bonus on the attacker side.

\textbf{1. Format Reward ($R_{\mathrm{fmt}}$).}
The attacker must emit a three-part response separating reasoning, strategy, and answer, which also yields a parseable strategy descriptor $b(y_A)\in\strategyspace$:
\begin{equation}
  R_{\mathrm{fmt}}(y_A) \;=\; \begin{cases}
    +r_{\mathrm{fmt}} & \text{if $y_A$ follows the prescribed schema and yields a valid } b(y_A),\\
    -r_{\mathrm{fmt}} & \text{otherwise.}
  \end{cases}
\end{equation}
$R_{\mathrm{fmt}}$ is applied only to the attacker, since requiring structured reasoning of the defender is known to degrade general capability~\citep{magic2025}.

\textbf{2. Harmfulness Reward ($R_{\mathrm{harm}}$).}
Qwen3Guard~\citep{zhao2025qwen3guard} serves as both the training-time reward model and the in-distribution safety judge; ``controversial'' responses are treated as unsafe to avoid rewarding ambiguous cases.
\begin{equation}
  R_{\mathrm{harm}}(y_D) \;=\; \begin{cases}
    +r_{\mathrm{harm}} & \text{if $y_D$ is labeled safe},\\
    -r_{\mathrm{harm}} & \text{if $y_D$ is labeled unsafe or controversial.}
  \end{cases}
\end{equation}

\textbf{3. Refusal Reward ($R_{\mathrm{ref}}$).}
To prevent the defender from collapsing into over-refusal, we use a refusal reward that jointly penalizes false refusals on benign prompts and false compliance with unsafe ones:
\begin{equation}
  R_{\mathrm{ref}}(x, y_D) \;=\; \begin{cases}
    +r_{\mathrm{ref}} & \text{if $x$ is unsafe and $y_D$ refuses,}\\
    -r_{\mathrm{ref}} & \text{if $x$ is unsafe and $y_D$ does not refuse,}\\
    -r_{\mathrm{ref}} & \text{if $x$ is safe and $y_D$ refuses,}\\
    +r_{\mathrm{ref}} & \text{if $x$ is safe and $y_D$ complies appropriately.}
  \end{cases}
\end{equation}

\textbf{4. Diversity Reward ($R_{\mathrm{div}}$).}
The normalized marginal coverage gain (Eq.~\ref{eq:coverage-gain}), gated by $\lambda(x)$ in Eq.~\ref{eq:lambda}, is applied only to the attacker---a strategy-level exploration pressure with no defender analogue.

\textbf{Final reward formulation.}
The effectiveness component $R_{\mathrm{harm}}+R_{\mathrm{ref}}$ is strictly zero-sum, while $R_{\mathrm{fmt}}$ and $\lambda(x)R_{\mathrm{div}}$ are attacker-side regularizers that make the overall game \emph{general-sum}:
\begin{align}
  R_D &\;=\; R_{\mathrm{harm}}(y_D) \;+\; R_{\mathrm{ref}}(x, y_D), \\
  R_A &\;=\; \underbrace{-R_D}_{\text{zero-sum effectiveness}} \;+\; R_{\mathrm{fmt}}(y_A) \;+\; \lambda(x)\cdot R_{\mathrm{div}}(x).
  \label{eq:reward-A}
\end{align}
The GRPO loss for $R_A$ and $R_D$ is standard and reproduced in Appendix~\ref{apdx:algorithm}.

For the SFT phase, we construct a strategy-conditioned CoT dataset in which every example requires the attacker to emit a reasoning trace, a strategy declaration $(s, c) \in \strategyspace$, and a rewritten prompt. For the RL phase, we sample 15{,}000 vanilla harmful prompts and 15{,}000 vanilla benign prompts from the WildJailBreak training split, which are rewritten by the attacker during training. \textbf{Baselines.}\quad

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

% [Conclusion] 单段落，主要陈述 contribution；limitation 与 future 各一句带过。
% 中文对应内容：本文提出 DACE，一个多样性驱动的对抗协同演化框架，从攻击者侧的结构化覆盖和防御者侧的贝叶斯对抗回放两个角度同时缓解"攻击策略坍缩"和"防御对抗性遗忘"两大病态，把协同演化从"双方交替进步"升级为"联合优化广度与持久性"。我们在多项基准上评估 DACE 的效果。局限方面，12×10 的策略空间是人工设计的离散网格，可能被未来攻击者绕过；我们仅在单轮文本设置下验证。未来工作可向自动化策略空间发现、多轮多模态安全对齐与跨训练周期的 lifelong 对抗记忆等方向延伸。
In this paper, we introduced \method, a diversity-driven adversarial co-evolution framework that jointly mitigates \emph{attack strategy collapse} and \emph{defense adversarial forgetting} by pairing an explicit $12\times10$ strategy space and a normalized marginal coverage gain reward on the attacker side with a unified Bayesian adversarial replay pool---equipped with Beta--Bernoulli threat posteriors, time decay, and Thompson sampling---on the defender side, so that co-evolution is jointly driven by strategy-level coverage and continuously refreshed threat tracking. We evaluate \method on a suite of safety, strong-attacker, multi-turn, and general-capability benchmarks. Our approach has limitations: the strategy space is a manually curated discrete grid that future attackers may eventually grow beyond, and our evaluation is limited to the single-turn text setting. Future work includes automated strategy-space discovery, extensions to multimodal and multi-turn agentic safety alignment, and a lifelong adversarial replay pool shared across training cycles.

\section{Full \method Training Algorithm}
\label{apdx:algorithm}

% [App A 引子]
% 中文对应内容：本附录给出 DACE 训练主循环的完整伪代码与超参对应表。算法 A.1 对应正文 §3.1 的 Phase 2 迭代协同演化流程，并补充了 time decay、Thompson 采样、posterior 更新和剪枝的每一步细节。表 A.1 罗列所有主实验默认超参及其在开源实现中的对应位置，方便一键复现。
Algorithm~\ref{alg:dace-full} gives the full pseudocode of \method, exposing all data structures, boundary cases, and the hyperparameters used in our main experiments. Table~\ref{tab:hyperparams} lists the corresponding hyperparameter values and the exact location at which each one is consumed in the released implementation.

\begin{algorithm}[h]
  \caption{\method training loop (full)}
  \label{alg:dace-full}
  \begin{algorithmic}[1]
    \REQUIRE attacker $\piA$ (SFT-warm-started), defender $\piD$ (base instruct), seed set $\mathcal{Q}$; rounds $K$, per-stage steps $T$, group size $G$, batch sizes $B_{\mathrm{train}},B_{\mathrm{replay}}$, decay $\gamma$, prior $(\alpha,\beta)$, prune thresholds $(\tau_{\mathrm{prune}}, n_{\min})$, smoothing $\varepsilon_{\mathrm{div}}$, diversity coefs $(\lambda_{\mathrm{succ}}, \lambda_{\mathrm{fail}})=(1.0, 0.5)$
    \STATE Initialize archive $\archive \leftarrow \emptyset$; strategy-count histogram $\mathbf{n}\in\mathbb{N}^{|\strategyspace|}\leftarrow\mathbf{0}$.
    \FOR{round $k=1,\dots,K$}
      \FOR{each $x_i \in \archive$}
        \STATE $s_i \leftarrow \gamma\,s_i;\ f_i \leftarrow \gamma\,f_i$ \hfill\textit{time decay of pseudo-counts}
      \ENDFOR
      \STATE \textit{\# Stage 1: Attacker optimization (fix $\piD$)}
      \FOR{step $t=1,\dots,T$}
        \STATE Sample seeds $\{q_j\}\sim\mathcal{Q}$; attacker generates $G$ structured rewrites per seed.
        \STATE Extract $b(x)$ by regex + fuzzy substring; obtain $\piD(x)$ and judge verdicts.
        \STATE Compute $R_{\mathrm{div}}(x)$ using Eq.~\ref{eq:coverage-gain} with the batch-shared denominator.
        \STATE Compute $\lambda(x)$ (Eq.~\ref{eq:lambda}); form $R_A$ (Eq.~\ref{eq:reward-A}).
        \STATE Update $\piA$ by GRPO on $R_A$ within each group.
        \STATE For every successful $x$ not already in $\archive$: insert entry with $(s{=}1, f{=}0)$; increment $\mathbf{n}[b(x)]$.
      \ENDFOR
      \STATE \textit{\# Stage 2: Defender optimization on mixed mini-batch (fix $\piA$)}
      \FOR{step $t=1,\dots,T$}
        \STATE \textit{Fresh attacks:} sample seeds $\{q_j\}$; attacker produces one rewrite per seed; let $\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{new}}$ be the resulting batch of $B_{\mathrm{train}}$ attacks.
        \STATE \textit{Replay attacks:} for each entry in $\archive$, draw $\tilde p_i \sim \mathrm{Beta}(\alpha+s_i,\beta+f_i)$; let $\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{replay}}\leftarrow \operatorname{Top-}B_{\mathrm{replay}}(\{\tilde p_i\})$ \hfill\textit{Eq.~\ref{eq:thompson}}
        \STATE Defender generates $G$ rollouts per attack in $\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{train}}\!=\!\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{new}}\cup\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{replay}}$; compute $R_D$.
        \STATE Update $\piD$ by GRPO on $R_D$ with the mixed mini-batch of size $B_{\mathrm{train}}+B_{\mathrm{replay}}$.
        \FOR{each $x_i \in \mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{replay}}$}
          \STATE If any defender rollout was judged unsafe, $s_i\leftarrow s_i+1$; else $f_i\leftarrow f_i+1$. \hfill\textit{conjugate update}
          \STATE Recompute $\hat p_i$ via Eq.~\ref{eq:posterior}.
          \IF{$\hat p_i < \tau_{\mathrm{prune}}$ \textbf{and} $s_i + f_i > n_{\min}$}
            \STATE Remove $x_i$ from $\archive$; decrement $\mathbf{n}[b(x_i)]$ \hfill\textit{conservative pruning}
          \ENDIF
        \ENDFOR
        \STATE For every new success in $\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{new}}$ not already in $\archive$, insert $(x, b(x), s{=}1, f{=}0)$; increment $\mathbf{n}$.
      \ENDFOR
    \ENDFOR
    \RETURN $\piA, \piD, \archive$.
  \end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

\subsection{GRPO objective and mixed-batch advantage estimation}
\label{apdx:grpo}

% [App A.1 · GRPO 目标函数 + 混合 batch 的 per-prompt advantage 说明]
% 中文对应内容：附录 A.1 给出 DACE 训练主循环中 GRPO 的完整目标函数并澄清一个工程关键点：在 defender 混合 batch 的 GRPO 更新下，advantage 是在每个 prompt 独立的 G 个 rollouts 内部归一化的，而不是在整个 batch 内跨 prompt 归一化。写作顺序：(1) 给出攻击者与防御者共享的 GRPO clipped surrogate loss；(2) 给出单 prompt 内 G 个候选响应的 advantage 公式；(3) 指出在混合 batch 中 D_new 与 D_replay 的样本难度存在系统差异（新攻击通常易通过、历史攻击通常更难），若跨 prompt 归一化会把难度差异吸收进 advantage 符号，导致"D_replay 的 rollouts 总是被推成正 advantage / D_new 的 rollouts 总是被推成负 advantage"的错误监督信号；(4) 我们严格按 per-prompt group 归一化，advantage 仅反映该 prompt 内部的相对好坏，天然免疫跨 prompt 难度扭曲；(5) 提一句实现参考 veRL 的 trainer.compute_grpo_advantages。
We expand the compact pseudocode of Algorithm~\ref{alg:dace-full} with the explicit GRPO surrogate loss and, crucially, with the advantage-estimation rule that \method uses on the defender's mixed mini-batch. This clarifies a common implementation question: how does group-relative advantage interact with a batch whose prompts are of systematically different difficulty (fresh attacks vs.\ replayed historical attacks)?

\paragraph{Shared GRPO objective.} Following~\citet{shao2024grpo, guo2025deepseek}, both the attacker update on $R_A$ and the defender update on $R_D$ minimise the clipped surrogate loss
\begin{equation}
  \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{GRPO}}(\theta) \;=\; -\,\mathbb{E}_{\tau}\!\left[\frac{1}{G}\sum_{i=1}^{G}\Big(\min\!\big(\rho_i\,\hat A_i,\ \mathrm{clip}(\rho_i,\,1{-}\epsilon,\,1{+}\epsilon)\,\hat A_i\big) \;-\; \beta\,\KL\!\big(\pi_\theta\,\|\,\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}\big)\Big)\right],
  \label{eq:grpo-loss}
\end{equation}
where $\rho_i = \pi_\theta(o_i\mid p)\,/\,\pi_{\mathrm{old}}(o_i\mid p)$ is the importance ratio for the $i$-th rollout $o_i$ generated from prompt $p$, $\hat A_i$ is its group-relative advantage, $\epsilon$ is the PPO-style clip range, and $\beta$ controls the KL anchor to the reference policy. For the attacker, $p$ is a seed query $q$ and $o_i$ is a rewrite $y_A^{(i)}$; for the defender, $p$ is an attack prompt $x_j\in\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{train}}$ and $o_i$ is a response $y_D^{(i)}$.

\paragraph{Per-prompt group-relative advantage.} \method estimates the advantage \emph{independently within each prompt's own $G$-rollout group}. Concretely, for each prompt $p$ in either $\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{new}}$ or $\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{replay}}$, the defender samples $G$ responses $\{y_D^{(i)}\}_{i=1}^G\sim\piD(\cdot\mid p)$, observes the per-response reward $r_i = R_D(p, y_D^{(i)})$, and sets
\begin{equation}
  \hat A_i(p) \;=\; \frac{r_i - \mu(p)}{\sigma(p) + \varepsilon_{\mathrm{adv}}},\qquad
  \mu(p) = \tfrac{1}{G}\textstyle\sum_{k=1}^{G} r_k,\qquad
  \sigma(p) = \sqrt{\tfrac{1}{G}\textstyle\sum_{k=1}^{G}(r_k - \mu(p))^2}.
  \label{eq:grpo-adv}
\end{equation}
Equation~\ref{eq:grpo-adv} is evaluated \emph{per prompt}---i.e.\ $\mu(p), \sigma(p)$ are computed from the $G$ rollouts that share that prompt, not from the entire $(B_{\mathrm{train}}+B_{\mathrm{replay}})\cdot G$-sized mini-batch. The attacker update uses the same rule with $r_i$ replaced by $R_A(y_A^{(i)}, y_D^{(i)})$ and $p$ replaced by the seed query.

\paragraph{Why this matters for the mixed batch.} Fresh attacks in $\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{new}}$ and replayed attacks in $\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{replay}}$ have \emph{systematically different expected reward distributions}: Thompson sampling biases $\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{replay}}$ toward high-threat entries whose expected $R_D$ is lower than that of typical fresh attacks, while $\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{new}}$ is dominated by the attacker's current easy-success modes. If advantages were instead normalised \emph{across} the full mini-batch, every rollout for a replayed prompt would tend to land below the global mean and receive a negative advantage, and every rollout for a fresh prompt would tend to land above the global mean and receive a positive advantage, regardless of \emph{within-prompt} quality. The policy gradient would then encode prompt difficulty rather than per-prompt response quality, contaminating both directions of learning. The per-prompt normalisation in Eq.~\ref{eq:grpo-adv} eliminates this confound: $\hat A_i(p)$ depends only on how the $i$-th response compares with its $G-1$ siblings for the same $p$, so fresh and replayed prompts contribute orthogonal learning signal to the shared GRPO update and the variance of the gradient estimator stays bounded by the within-group scale.

\paragraph{Implementation note.} In veRL~\citep{sheng2025verl}, this is the default behaviour of \texttt{compute\_grpo\_advantages} when called with prompt-id grouping enabled; \method inherits this setting without modification. The mixed mini-batch is assembled at the dataloader level (\texttt{trainer.ppo.ray\_trainer}) so that rollouts belonging to the same prompt remain contiguous when advantages are scattered back to the loss.

\subsection{KL-contraction interpretation of the normalized coverage gain}
\label{apdx:kl-contraction}

% [App A.1 · KL 视角完整推导] 中文对应内容：附录 A.1 给出正文 §3.3 中 KL 视角推导的完整形式。利用 H(p) = log|B| - D_KL(p||u)，原始边际熵增等价于加入样本前后 p_A 到均匀分布 u 的 KL 散度减少量：H(p_{A∪x}) - H(p_A) = D_KL(p_A||u) - D_KL(p_{A∪x}||u)。因此归一化覆盖增益 R_div 的含义是"该样本把 p_A 向 u 收缩的 KL 减少量占当前档案状态下单样本可达最大 KL 减少量的比例"（正截断除外）。两个对偶性质直接由 KL 散度的基本事实得到：(i) 对第 b 槽位概率的梯度 ∂ D_KL(p||u) / ∂ p_b = 1 + log p_b - log u_b 在低频槽位取得最大的下降，因此最大化 R_div 等价于沿 KL 最陡下降方向选择策略；(ii) 前向 KL 的 zero-avoiding 性质意味着 p_b → 0 时 KL → ∞，从而空白槽位对应最强的探索压力，与覆盖目标——不遗漏任何策略区域——完全一致。
Using the identity $H(\mathbf{p}) = \log|\strategyspace| - \KL(\mathbf{p}\,\|\,\uniform)$ with $\uniform$ the uniform prior over the $120$-cell strategy space, the raw marginal entropy gain in Eq.~\ref{eq:coverage-gain} rewrites as
\begin{equation}
  H(\mathbf{p}_{\archive\cup\{x\}}) - H(\archivedist)
  \;=\; \KL(\archivedist\,\|\,\uniform) - \KL(\mathbf{p}_{\archive\cup\{x\}}\,\|\,\uniform),
  \label{eq:kl-identity}
\end{equation}
i.e., the single-step KL reduction toward $\uniform$. Before the positive clip in Eq.~\ref{eq:coverage-gain}, the normalized coverage gain $R_{\mathrm{div}}(x)$ is therefore the fraction of one-step KL contraction that $x$ achieves, relative to the best attainable single-sample contraction at the current archive state. Two properties follow directly from elementary facts about $\KL$:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*, topsep=2pt, itemsep=2pt]
  \item \textbf{Steepest-descent direction.} The partial derivative of $\KL(\mathbf{p}\,\|\,\uniform)$ with respect to the mass on the $b$-th cell is $\partial_{p_b}\KL(\mathbf{p}\,\|\,\uniform) = 1 + \log p_b - \log u_b$, whose magnitude grows as $p_b \downarrow 0$. Selecting an under-populated cell therefore yields the steepest per-sample decrease in KL, which is the exact quantity the normalizer in Eq.~\ref{eq:coverage-gain} maximizes over $b$; so $R_{\mathrm{div}}(x) \to 1$ whenever $x$ lands in the currently sparsest cell.
  \item \textbf{Zero-avoiding pressure.} Forward $\KL$ diverges as any $p_b \to 0$; consequently, cells that have never been visited carry the largest per-sample KL reduction potential, and $R_{\mathrm{div}}$ places the strongest exploration pressure on empty cells. This matches the coverage objective of Definition~\ref{def:collapse}---no strategy region should be structurally omitted---and complements the discrete grid by guaranteeing that even deep into training the attacker receives a non-vanishing signal to visit previously neglected regions.
\end{itemize}
The per-batch overhead of evaluating the normalizer is a single $\mathcal{O}(|\strategyspace|)=\mathcal{O}(120)$ sweep over cells, amortized across all samples in the group.

\subsection{Bayesian adversarial replay: expanded details}
\label{apdx:bayesian-replay}

% [App A.2 · Bayesian replay 补充] 中文对应内容：附录 A.2 扩展正文 §3.4 的细节。(a) 后验方差 Var(p_i) = (α+s_i)(β+f_i) / ((α+β+s_i+f_i)^2 (α+β+s_i+f_i+1))——试验次数越多 variance 越小，自然量化置信度；(b) 时间衰减的信息论解读：衰减因子 γ 对应有效样本量 N_eff = (s_i+f_i)/(1-γ)，等价于在 N_eff 步后权重降到初始的 1/e；(c) Thompson 采样的收敛性：当 posterior 收敛到 p_i 的 delta 分布时，Top-B_replay 采样退化为确定性 top-B_replay，与 upper-confidence bandit 方法的渐近行为一致；(d) 剪枝的最坏情形分析：在 τ_prune=0.45, n_min=2 下，最早可能剪枝发生在 (s=0, f=3) 或 (s=1, f=4)，对应后验均值 1/5 = 0.20 或 2/7 ≈ 0.286，均安全地位于 τ_prune 以下。
The main text (Section~\ref{sec:method-replay}) gives a compact description of the replay mechanism. Here we record the expanded derivations, closed forms, and corner-case analyses underpinning its default settings.

\paragraph{Posterior variance and uncertainty quantification.}
The closed-form variance of the Beta posterior in Eq.~\ref{eq:posterior} is
\begin{equation}
  \mathrm{Var}(p_i \mid s_i, f_i) \;=\; \frac{(\alpha+s_i)(\beta+f_i)}{(\alpha+\beta+s_i+f_i)^2\,(\alpha+\beta+s_i+f_i + 1)},
  \label{eq:posterior-var}
\end{equation}
which is monotonically decreasing in the total trial count $s_i+f_i$. Entries with few trials therefore carry high variance by construction, and the Thompson-sampling step naturally re-examines them; entries with many trials concentrate near $\hat p_i$ and are selected deterministically. This is the structural advantage over the discrete-score update of~\citet{ssp2026}, which conflates few-trial and many-trial estimates.

\paragraph{Effective sample size under exponential time decay.}
The exponential discount $(s_i, f_i)\leftarrow(\gamma s_i, \gamma f_i)$ is equivalent to a sliding exponential window whose effective sample size is $N_{\mathrm{eff}} = 1/(1-\gamma)$; with $\gamma=0.90$, $N_{\mathrm{eff}}=10$ rounds, so evidence older than roughly ten rounds contributes less than $1/e$ of its original weight. Non-integer pseudo-counts remain well-defined because the Beta distribution is parameterized by arbitrary positive reals, and the conjugate update is unchanged.

\paragraph{Worst-case pruning analysis.}
With the default $\tau_{\mathrm{prune}}=0.45$ and $n_{\min}=2$, the earliest a pruning event can occur is at $(s_i, f_i)=(0, 3)$ or $(1, 4)$ (modulo time-decay fractions), giving $\hat p_i = 1/5=0.20$ or $\hat p_i=2/7\approx 0.286$---both safely below the threshold. Thus a single lucky refusal can \emph{never} prune an entry, and low-trial entries with sustained high posterior mean are explicitly protected. The converse bias---retaining too many low-threat entries---is controlled by the archive capacity (Table~\ref{tab:hyperparams}), after which an LRU-by-mean policy caps memory consumption.

\paragraph{Thompson sampling limits.}
As the posteriors concentrate ($N_{\mathrm{eff}} \uparrow$ without new evidence), each $\tilde p_i$ degenerates to its mean $\hat p_i$, so the Top-$B_{\mathrm{replay}}$ procedure reduces to a deterministic top-$B_{\mathrm{replay}}$ selection, matching the asymptotic behaviour of upper-confidence-bound bandits. In the opposite limit (all entries fresh), Thompson sampling is maximally exploratory and $\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{replay}}$ is an approximately uniform sub-sample of the archive, so the replay schedule adapts continuously between exploration and exploitation without an explicit schedule hyperparameter.

\section{Training Setup}
\label{apdx:training-setup}

% 中文对应内容：附录 D 记录硬件配置、随机种子协议、训练 wall-clock 与节点信息。我们在 8×H100 节点上使用 veRL 框架运行 DACE 全流程；所有主表数字以 3 个随机种子为平均单位汇报，种子内方差在 Appendix F 的扩展结果中给出。为了与 MAGIC / Self-RedTeam 进行公平对比，每个 baseline 使用与 DACE 相同的 SFT 数据、同一 defender 基模和同一判官组合。SFT 蒸馏 attacker 使用 Gemini-2.5-Pro；每个 RL 轮的 wall-clock 在 H100 8卡节点上约为 \{pending\} 分钟。Phase 2 采用 ratio-based agent switching（attacker/defender 比例 1:1，频率 15 步）。
We run \method and all co-evolution baselines on a single 8$\times$H100 node using veRL~\citep{sheng2025verl} with GRPO as the inner optimizer. Main-table numbers are reported as the mean across three independent training seeds; per-seed standard deviations appear in Appendix~\ref{apdx:expanded-results}. The attacker SFT corpus is distilled by Gemini-2.5-Pro; the in-training safety judge is Qwen3Guard. Co-evolution uses the ratio-based agent-switching schedule from MAGIC (1:1 attacker:defender ratio, update frequency 15 steps). We checkpoint after every round so that the cross-evaluation matrix of Appendix~\ref{apdx:expanded-results} and the forgetting curves of Appendix~\ref{apdx:training-dynamics} can be rendered. Compute accounting (per-round wall-clock, total tokens, total GPU-hours) is summarized in Table~\ref{tab:hyperparams} and will be finalized alongside the released training logs.

% E.1 Safety benchmarks
% ----------------------------------------------------------
\subsection{Safety benchmarks (Tab.~\ref{tab:evaluation_main}).}
\label{apdx:bench-safety}

% 中文对应内容：以下七个静态安全基准覆盖 adversarial harmful / vanilla harmful / adversarial benign / vanilla benign 四类分布。我们统一在 Qwen3Guard 的 safety moderator 上汇报 ASR 与 RTA，并在附录 F.5 中附上 GPT-4o / Llama-Guard-4 的交叉验证。
\paragraph{WildGuardTest~\citep{han2024wildguard}.} A held-out split from the WildGuard corpus with both vanilla and adversarial harmful prompts. We use the official adversarial-harm and vanilla-harm subsets and report ASR judged by Qwen3Guard.

\paragraph{WildJailBreak (WJB)~\citep{jiang2024wildteaming}.} We use two of the WJB evaluation splits: the \emph{adversarial-harmful} split (advharm) for harmful ASR and the \emph{adversarial-benign} split (advbenign) for over-refusal ASR. The benign split measures whether a model still complies with prompts designed to look harmful but in fact are not.

\paragraph{HarmBench~\citep{mazeika2024harmbench}.} We use both the vanilla and adversarial splits of the HarmBench test set. The adversarial split contains prompts targeted at extracting harmful text generations and serves as our main battleground for OOD automated attackers (Tab.~\ref{tab:harmbench_generalization}).

\paragraph{DAN~\citep{shen2023doanythingnow}.} A curated collection of in-the-wild jailbreak templates popularized on online forums. We report ASR on the harmful subset.

\paragraph{OR-Bench-Toxic~\citep{cui2024orbench}.} The toxic subset of OR-Bench; used to measure Robustness-to-Attacks (RTA) of the defender, i.e.\ the fraction of prompts where the defender correctly refuses unsafe content.

\paragraph{XSTest~\citep{rottger2023xstest}.} We use both the \emph{unsafe} contrast set (as a harmful-refusal RTA test) and the \emph{safe} set (as a benign-compliance test, reported as \emph{Comply}). XSTest is the canonical diagnostic for exaggerated safety / over-refusal.

\paragraph{StrongREJECT~\citep{souly2024strongreject}.} A vanilla-harmful benchmark that measures whether the defender strongly rejects policy-violating requests; we report RTA.

\subsection{OOD automated red-teaming attackers (Tab.~\ref{tab:harmbench_generalization}).}
\label{apdx:bench-openrt}

% 中文对应内容：这一节介绍 Table 3 列中 GPT-4o 打分的六个 OOD attacker（no-rev 直接基线 + GCG/PAIR/TAP/AutoDAN/AutoDAN-Turbo/MAJIC）。新增的 MAJIC 需要额外段落，因它代表最近发布的 composition-heavy 黑盒 attacker，与 DACE 训练中涌现的组合攻击最相似。
\paragraph{no-rev (No Rewrite).} The direct ASR of the target defender on HarmBench prompts without any attacker-side rewriting. Serves as a lower bound against which OOD attackers must improve.

\paragraph{GCG~\citep{zou2023universal}.} Gradient-based adversarial suffix optimization on an open-weights surrogate; the produced suffix is transferred to the target defender.

\paragraph{PAIR~\citep{chao2023pair}.} An iterative LLM-as-attacker baseline: a strong attacker LLM refines the jailbreak prompt across up to 20 rounds using only the target's responses, without access to its weights.

\paragraph{TAP~\citep{mehrotra2024tap}.} A tree-of-attacks extension of PAIR that expands into multiple branches at each round and prunes low-quality subtrees, increasing coverage at a controlled query cost.

\paragraph{AutoDAN~\citep{liu2023autodan}.} A genetic / evolutionary attacker that mutates seed jailbreak prompts toward higher ASR under a population of candidate sentences.

\paragraph{AutoDAN-Turbo~\citep{liu2024autodan}.} A lifelong agent that maintains a growing library of attack strategies and chooses among them via an upper-confidence-style scheduler; represents the state of the art within the OpenRT default attacker suite before MAJIC.

\paragraph{MAJIC~\citep{qi2025majic} \emph{(new, added for \method)}.} Markovian Adaptive Jailbreaking via Iterative Composition of Diverse Innovative Strategies. MAJIC alternates between proposing candidate strategies and composing them through a Markovian plan graph, reportedly achieving $\approx 96\%$ ASR on Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct within $<\!15$ queries and representing the strongest publicly known composition-heavy black-box attacker at submission time. We add MAJIC to Tab.~\ref{tab:harmbench_generalization} as an especially stringent OOD test for \method.

\paragraph{Judge robustness cross-validation.} We re-score the main-table rows with Qwen3Guard, GPT-4o, and Llama-Guard-4 and report the Kendall $\tau$ between method rankings under the three judges, as well as the top-1 agreement rate. \section{Extended Related Work}
\label{apdx:related-work-full}

% 中文对应内容：按用户要求，正文不再保留 Related Work 章节；完整版放在本附录，分四个小节：jailbreak / 静态对齐 / 自动红队 / 协同演化 / 回放与持续学习。我们在前三节简述领域演化，第四节给出与 DACE 最接近的协同演化先验工作及其与 DACE 的结构性差异（见 Table I.1）。
We provide the full version of the related-work discussion that was elided from the main paper.

\paragraph{I.1~~Jailbreaks and static alignment.}
Jailbreak research has progressed from hand-crafted templates~\citep{shen2023doanythingnow} through gradient-based optimization~\citep{zou2023universal}, LLM-driven iterative rewriting~\citep{chao2023pair, mehrotra2024tap, liu2023autodan, liu2024autodan}, to multi-turn agentic attacks~\citep{rahman2025xteaming, qi2025majic, samvelyan2024rainbow, liu2024flipattack}. Defensive approaches dominated by RLHF~\citep{ouyang2022training, bai2022constitutional, dai2023safe} and fixed-distribution red-teaming data~\citep{jiang2024wildteaming, mazeika2024harmbench, rottger2023xstest, cui2024orbench, souly2024strongreject, xie2024sorry} are structurally tied to pre-collected attack distributions and thus brittle under drift.

\paragraph{I.2~~Automated red-teaming.}
A rich literature develops attacker algorithms across paradigms: gradient/optimization (GCG~\citep{zou2023universal}, AutoDAN~\citep{liu2023autodan}), LLM-as-attacker iterative rewriting (PAIR~\citep{chao2023pair}, TAP~\citep{mehrotra2024tap}, AutoDAN-Turbo~\citep{liu2024autodan}), multi-turn or agentic attackers (X-Teaming~\citep{rahman2025xteaming}, MAJIC~\citep{qi2025majic}), and scenario-shift or many-shot attacks (FlipAttack~\citep{liu2024flipattack}). These works typically treat the defender as fixed; \method integrates a diverse attacker inside a co-training loop.

\paragraph{I.3~~Diversity in red-teaming.}
Diversity-oriented red-teaming splits into the QD family and the RL-intrinsic family. QD methods---Rainbow Teaming~\citep{samvelyan2024rainbow}, Ruby Teaming~\citep{han2024ruby}, Ferret~\citep{pala2025ferret}, RainbowPlus~\citep{dang2025rainbowplus}, QDRT~\citep{wang2025qdrt}, T-MAP~\citep{guo2025tmap}, GRTS~\citep{grts2023}, Auto-RT~\citep{autort2025}, and the WildTeaming / RedTWIZ pipelines~\citep{jiang2024wildteaming, horal2025redtwiz}---all build behavior-descriptor archives against a static target. RL-intrinsic methods---CRT~\citep{hong2024curiosity}, DiveR-CT~\citep{zhao2025diverct}, GFlowNet-based attackers~\citep{lee2025gflownet}, Jailbreak-R1~\citep{wang2025jailbreakr1}, CALM~\citep{zheng2025calm}, RedTopic~\citep{redtopic2025}, OpenAI RBR~\citep{beutel2024openaidiverse}, Active Attacks~\citep{activeattacks2025}---operate at the token or embedding level. \method departs from both by placing diversity at the strategy level inside a dynamic co-evolution loop.

\paragraph{I.4~~Co-evolutionary safety alignment.}
Self-RedTeam~\citep{liu2025chasing} introduces zero-sum self-play with a Nash equilibrium; MAGIC~\citep{magic2025} casts the interaction as an asymmetric sequential game with SPNE; further variants include AdvEvo-MARL~\citep{pan2025advevo}, ACE-Safety~\citep{acesafety2025}, SEAS~\citep{seas2025}, SSP~\citep{ssp2026}, TriPlay-RL~\citep{triplayrl2026}, and several Stackelberg / non-cooperative formulations~\citep{twoplayergame2024, advgame2025}. \paragraph{I.5~~Experience replay and non-stationary bandits.}
\method's Bayesian replay mechanism draws on two complementary traditions: experience replay in continual RL~\citep{lin2020extrapolation} and non-stationary Thompson sampling in bandit theory~\citep{chapelle2011thompson}. Our adaptation is to treat each archived attack as a Bernoulli arm whose latent threat probability drifts with the defender, and to propagate drift through discounted pseudo-counts so that the replay selection step doubles as a Bayesian re-estimation step.

\section{Limitations, Broader Impact, and Ethical Considerations}
\label{apdx:limitations}

% 中文对应内容：在本附录中我们展开讨论正文 Conclusion 中一笔带过的三类局限。(1) 策略空间的离散与人工性：12×10 网格虽有明确数据支撑，但无法覆盖跨类别或细粒度亚策略，未来可探索自动发现或连续策略空间。(2) Judge 依赖与偏置传播：DACE 的 coverage 与 replay 都消费安全 judge；若 judge 对某些风险类别系统性低估，则协同演化可能放大该偏置。附录 F 的三 judge 交叉验证用来降低（但不能完全消除）这一风险。(3) 仅文本单轮验证：扩展到多模态、工具使用与多轮 agent 尚需额外实验。Broader Impact 与 Safeguards：我们释出 defender 权重、训练代码与 sanitized 策略索引回放池，不释出 attacker 权重；完整 archive 的访问须签署 non-misuse agreement。Ethical considerations：实验使用公开 benchmark 与合成 prompt，不涉及真实用户数据。
\paragraph{Limitations.}
First, the $12\times10$ strategy space is a manually curated discrete grid; while our ablations indicate the pruned grid is well-justified for a text-only defender, future attackers may exploit behaviors that straddle categories or lie outside the grid, and automated / continuous strategy spaces are a natural next step. Second, both the coverage and replay signals consume a safety judge; any systematic bias of the judge may be amplified by co-evolution. The three-judge cross-validation reported in Appendix~\ref{apdx:expanded-results} mitigates but does not eliminate this risk. Third, our evaluation is confined to text-only, single-turn interaction; extending \method to multimodal, tool-using, and multi-turn agentic settings is left to future work.

\paragraph{Broader impact and safeguards.}
\method is designed to strengthen safety alignment, but the training process inevitably generates higher-quality, more diverse adversarial prompts that could, in principle, be misused. We adopt three safeguards: (i) the public release includes the defender weights, training code, and evaluation infrastructure, but \emph{not} the attacker weights; (ii) the released archive is ethically sanitized to preserve structural composition patterns while redacting operational details; (iii) access to the un-sanitized archive is gated behind a non-misuse agreement.

\paragraph{Ethical considerations.}
All experiments use publicly released benchmarks and synthetically generated prompts; no real user data or personally identifying information is involved. Training and evaluation are performed on institutional compute. \method's attacker is not to be used against production systems without explicit authorization.
